# REX AI Enablement Strategy Visualization

## Product Purpose
Internal strategy visualization for IKEA REX engineering teams. Communicates the AI enablement initiative: what it is, how it's structured, what skills to build, how the dev lifecycle changes, and how to teach it. The design IS the deliverable.

## Users
Engineering leads, developers, and team leads across IKEA REX who need to understand and adopt AI-native development practices. Viewed on laptops in conference rooms during team meetings and presentations.

## Brand Voice
Structured, authoritative, energizing. Like a well-designed transit system map crossed with a strategic briefing deck. Clear enough for a developer seeing this for the first time, compelling enough to drive action.

## Register
brand

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards with hero metrics
- Corporate slide decks with bullet points
- AI-purple-gradient tech landing pages
- Infographics that look like they came from Canva
